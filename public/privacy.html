Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Tasks is a simple todo list. This page explains, in plain language, what it stores and why.

What we store

  • Your Google account name, email address and profile photo URL, provided by Google when you sign in.
  • The content you create: tasks, tags, deadlines and your completion history.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) in an EU multi-region location.

Why we store it

Only to provide the app: to sign you in and to keep your list in sync across your devices. We show no ads, we never sell your data, and we use no tracking or analytics.

Who can access it

Only you. Access rules isolate each account, so one person's list can never be read by another account.

Export and deletion

You can export your data at any time from the app's settings menu. To delete your account and all of your data, contact the developer at the address below.
