# #67 Phone Week view opens at Monday; today can be screens away
Status: done
PR: pending
Summary: Below 1024px, opening Week (tab, palette, or a reload in Week) on the current week scrolls instantly so today's header sits on its sticky line under the phone bars, or 16px from the top on a tablet. Other weeks and the 7-column desktop layout are unchanged. The phone day headers were already `position: sticky` under the bars, and e2e now checks they stay pinned and uncovered.

## What changed
- src/components/WeekView.tsx: a mount-only effect (`anchor === null`, `max-width: 1023px`, today isn't the first day) does `window.scrollBy` by the header's distance from its computed sticky `top`, with `behavior: 'instant'`, so there is no smooth scroll under any motion setting.
- e2e/week-phone.spec.ts: at 375, today's header lands under the bars, stays pinned while scrolling and isn't covered. At 800 it lands near the top. At 1280, opening Week doesn't scroll. Both tests skip on a Monday.

## Tried and rejected
- `scrollIntoView` with `scroll-margin-top` on the day: the header's own top margin left it 8px under the sticky line.

## Notes for future agents
- The issue said headers scroll away. At 375 in Chromium they stick (top `--sticky-2`); I couldn't reproduce that, so I changed no CSS. If it shows up on a real iPhone, look for an ancestor with `overflow` other than visible.
- On a tablet the page must be long enough to scroll; with little below today it stops at the page end.
