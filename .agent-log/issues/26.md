# #26 Touch targets of at least 44 px on phone
Status: done
PR: #60
Summary: Adds `e2e/touch-targets.spec.ts` (mobile-chromium, 375 and 320px), which walks `elementFromPoint` out from each control's centre and asserts ≥44×44; it also folds in #65, fixing the four controls it caught: Search (`--tap-inset` so the border no longer eats 2px), "Move to today" (12px under the overdue line on coarse phones, since its wrapper clips), the note icon (8px after it) and the editor ⋯ (moved 10px left of the difficulty button).

## What changed
- e2e/touch-targets.spec.ts: soft asserts for Search, Move to today, checkbox, difficulty, tag, note, editor ⋯ and the Week "+". A screen edge counts as part of a target. Sampling is in 0.5px steps, so the bar is 43.5.
- src/test/e2eHook.ts, e2e/global.d.ts: `__e2e.setNotes(title, notes)`, like `setDueDate`.
- OverdueNudge.tsx: no height animation under reduced motion. On CI the spec measured "Move to today" while its wrapper was still growing and clipping it (0.5×0.5).
- SearchButton, OverdueNudge and TaskItem `.module.css`: the fixes above, all in phone/coarse blocks. Desktop is untouched.

## Tried and rejected
- Bigger hit boxes alone for note/⋯ and Move to today: the later sibling's hit area (tag, difficulty) or the next row's title band wins `elementFromPoint`, so only spacing frees the room.
- `overflow: visible` on the nudge after its animation: the hit box would still sit under the first row's title band.
